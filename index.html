FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Tap any question below to view answers instantly

Do you offer discounts to coaches and schools?
Yes! We run special promotions from time to time. Eligible coaches and schools may benefit from discounts or promotional rewards when purchasing qualifying quantities. Contact us for the latest offers and conditions.
Can I place a bulk order for my school or team?
Absolutely! We welcome bulk orders from schools, clubs, and sporting organisations. Contact us with your required quantities, sizes, and product preferences for assistance with your order.
Do you deliver islandwide in Jamaica?
We aim to make ordering convenient for customers across Jamaica. Contact us to confirm delivery arrangements, availability, and the expected delivery time for your location.
How do I place an order?
You can order through our website or contact us directly via WhatsApp. Please provide the product name, brand, size, quantity, and your contact information so we can assist you.
What sizes do you have available?
Sizes vary according to the product and current stock. Please contact us with the type of footwear, brand, and size you need so we can confirm availability.
Do you sell shoes to schools and teams?
Yes! We work with schools, coaches, clubs, and individual athletes. We welcome bulk orders and can help you source footwear for your school's track and field, netball, football, and other sporting programmes.
How much do your products cost?
Our prices vary depending on the brand, style, and size. We aim to offer options for different budgets. Contact us or browse our online store to see current prices and available promotions.
What products does Prince Athletics sell?
We offer a range of athletic footwear, including track and field spikes, running shoes, netball sneakers, football boots, throwing shoes, and selected sportswear and accessories. Our goal is to provide athletes with quality sporting products at affordable prices.
